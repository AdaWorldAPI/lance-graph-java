Reading the table:
  * The two *Payload rows are the OPTIMISTIC lower bound for Ghidra's real
    types ? every reference deleted. The real Varnode additionally holds an
    Address, and the real PcodeOp a SequenceNumber, a Varnode[] and a Varnode.
  * A 2-input PcodeOp is therefore FIVE heap objects: the op, its
    SequenceNumber, the input array, and one Varnode per operand.

  refs flat: 3/3    payloads flat: 0/2

  VERDICT: the facade must ADDRESS the vocabulary, not CARRY it.
  Ordinals flatten; Ghidra's payloads do not, even at their lower bound.
  This is the same result LaneId/Ordinal/MaskId already rely on, and the
  same reason RowRange (16 B) does not flatten. Nothing new is needed ?
  the existing descriptor discipline already answers it.
